# References

The framework definitions implemented here come from the following works. Please obtain the
originals from the publishers; they are not redistributed in this repository.

1. Lahnala, A., Neuendorf, B., Thomin, A., Welch, C., Stibane, T., & Flek, L. (2024).
   *Appraisal Framework for Clinical Empathy: A Novel Application to Breaking Bad News
   Conversations.* Proceedings of LREC-COLING 2024.
2. Pounds, G. (2011). Empathy as 'appraisal': A new language-based approach to the analysis of
   clinical discourse. *Text & Talk*, 31(2), 139-162.
3. Baile, W. F., Buckman, R., Lenzi, R., Glober, G., Beale, E. A., & Kudelka, A. P. (2000).
   SPIKES - A six-step protocol for delivering bad news. *The Oncologist*, 5(4), 302-311.

`docs/BBN_Framework_Summary.md` is a paraphrased working summary, not a copy of any of the above.
